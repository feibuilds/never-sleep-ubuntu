# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free project. Be constructive, assume good intent while still discussing technical evidence critically, and respect differences in experience, identity, and viewpoint.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Reports can be made privately through the maintainer contact on their GitHub profile. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
